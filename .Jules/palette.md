## 2026-05-25 - Tooltips for specialized AAMVA standards
**Learning:** For forms involving specialized industry standards (like AAMVA), providing localized tooltips for technical acronyms (DCS, DAC, etc.) significantly improves accessibility for non-expert users without cluttering the UI.
**Action:** Always look for technical jargon or acronyms in form labels and provide explanatory tooltips or help text.

## 2026-05-25 - Accessible labels for icon-only buttons
**Learning:** For icon-only buttons in Streamlit, using the `help` parameter not only provides a visual tooltip but also acts as an accessible label for screen readers, improving the overall a11y score.
**Action:** Use the `help` parameter on all icon-only buttons to ensure they have a descriptive text alternative.
