## 2025-06-08 - Tooltips for Technical Acronyms

**Learning:** When dealing with specialized industry standards (like AAMVA for driver's licenses), technical acronyms (DCG, DAC, etc.) can be a major barrier for non-expert users. Adding descriptive tooltips significantly improves accessibility without cluttering the UI.

**Action:** Always check if industry-specific technical codes are used in the UI and provide localized human-readable explanations via tooltips or helper text.
