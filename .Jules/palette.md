## 2026-06-02 - Localized AAMVA Tooltips
**Learning:** For specialized industry standards (like AAMVA), providing localized tooltips for technical acronyms (DCG, DAC, etc.) significantly improves accessibility for non-expert users without cluttering the UI.
**Action:** Always look for technical acronyms in the UI and provide explanatory tooltips, ensuring they are integrated into the existing localization system.
