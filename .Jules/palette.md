## 2025-05-15 - [AAMVA Technical Acronym Accessibility]
**Learning:** For forms involving specialized industry standards (like AAMVA), labels are often technical acronyms (DCG, DAC, etc.) that are non-intuitive for non-expert users. Localized tooltips provide essential guidance without cluttering the UI.
**Action:** Always provide localized `help` text for technical field acronyms to ensure accessibility and clarity for all users.
