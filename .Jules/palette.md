## 2025-05-22 - Enhancing Accessibility with Specialized Tooltips

**Learning:** For forms involving specialized industry standards (like AAMVA), providing localized tooltips for technical acronyms significantly improves accessibility for non-expert users without cluttering the UI. High-contrast themes like 'Liquid Glass' require careful consideration of tooltip content readability.

**Action:** Always provide descriptive tooltips for technical field labels and ensure interactive elements like theme toggles have ARIA-friendly labels or tooltips to guide the user.
