## 2025-05-22 - [Localized tooltips for technical acronyms]
**Learning:** For forms involving specialized industry standards (like AAMVA), providing localized tooltips for technical acronyms significantly improves accessibility for non-expert users without cluttering the UI.
**Action:** Always check for technical codes or industry-specific jargon in forms and provide descriptive, localized tooltips (using Streamlit's `help` parameter or equivalent) to ensure the interface is intuitive and accessible.
