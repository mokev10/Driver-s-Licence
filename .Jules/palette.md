## 2025-05-15 - Improving Accessibility for Technical Acronyms
**Learning:** In specialized domains like AAMVA identity generation, technical acronyms (DCG, DAC, etc.) are common but opaque to non-experts. Using Streamlit's `help` parameter to provide localized tooltips significantly improves accessibility without cluttering the "Liquid Glass" theme's minimalist aesthetic.
**Action:** Always provide descriptive tooltips for industry-specific codes or jargon in user-facing forms.
