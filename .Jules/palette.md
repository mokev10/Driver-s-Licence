## 2025-05-15 - Enhancing Technical Field Clarity with Tooltips
**Learning:** In applications dealing with industry-standard but cryptic acronyms (like AAMVA codes), users benefit significantly from contextual tooltips. In Streamlit, the `help` parameter provides an accessible way to deliver this info without cluttering the UI.
**Action:** Always provide descriptive tooltips for technical acronyms and use `label_visibility="collapsed"` with a descriptive label for icon-only inputs to maintain screen reader accessibility.
