## 2025-05-15 - [Technical Acronyms Accessibility]
**Learning:** In technical domains like AAMVA barcode generation, cryptic acronyms (DCG, DAC, DCS) pose a significant cognitive barrier for non-expert users. While the labels include descriptive names, detailed tooltips provided via Streamlit's `help` parameter significantly improve the "discoverability" of field requirements without cluttering the UI.
**Action:** Always provide descriptive tooltips for technical field identifiers, and ensure these tooltips are localized to maintain accessibility across all supported languages.
